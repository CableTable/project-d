| 2026-10-18 | Join gaps: what moves | Ends within the tolerance of each other (not already joined: ends within 2e-6 units of each other count as joined, because a saved file keeps 6 decimals) form a cluster; the ends of a cluster all go to its mean, which for a pair is the midpoint.
