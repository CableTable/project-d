Round 10 · Auto-nest

Mock-up only, nothing is built. Method (his pick, 2026-10-19): parts are packed by their real outline, not their bounding box, turned in 90° steps, filled from the bottom-left corner, keeping the layout's gap and grippers, on the current layout's sheet. Both options are a real frame of the app (the Layout tab, sheet 600 × 420, four parts from the Parts library: Bracket L, Plate A, Dial, Tab); the nesting shown was worked out with a small stand-in packer, so the numbers are honest, but the app's own will be different. Auto-nest is the button that is greyed today (rail, next to Step and repeat).

What it doesRun packs 46 pieces (8 × Bracket L, 16 × Plate A, 12 × Dial, 10 × Tab). 44 of 46 fit (76 % of the printable sheet), 2 × Dial are left over, listed, with a one-click "next sheet". Rotations: 0° only · 0° + 180° · every 90° (default). Gap and gripper are the layout's own, not new settings. Ctrl+Z takes the whole run back in one step.

A · A panel section

The greyed Auto-nest button opens a section at the top of the Layout panel. The result is on the sheet at once; Undo takes it back.

      The Auto-nest… button, the rail tool and the nest command open this dialog on a layout tab. The packer is exact on the part outlines (real shapes, 0 / 180 / every 90°, bottom-left first, largest first) and runs in a Web Worker.

      On the boardAs built
      Header: "Auto-nest", layout · sheet · gripper · gap, close ×matches
      Left: hint line, PARTS AND HOW MANY, a row per part (thumbnail, name, size, − count +), "N pieces from M parts" + Set all to 1matches (counts can be typed as well as stepped)
      ROTATIONS: 0° only | 0° + 180° | Every 90° (default Every 90°); "Gap between parts" with the layout's value, read-onlymatches
      Right: PREVIEW · NOT APPLIED YET (PLACING… while it runs); sheet with the gripper band and label, parts coloured by kind, legendmatches; parts already on the sheet are drawn grey and kept as obstacles
      Result: "N of M placed", "% of the sheet used", Left over (n): k × name, "Nest the left-overs on the next sheet"matches; the % is of the usable sheet and counts parts already on it
      Footer: Cancel, Run again, Apply (primary); running: Placing n of M… and time, progress bar, Stop, Apply greyedmatches; before the first run the footer has Cancel and Run
      Apply = one undo step "Auto-nest"; Cancel and Run again leave the sheet alone; the new parts are selected after Applyas designed
      "Next sheet": applies this nest, opens a new layout tab (same sheet, grippers, gap, the left-over parts) and runs the dialog on it at onceas designed
      Differences on purposebefore the first Run the preview is an empty sheet (no sample); parts are packed flush to the usable edges, the gap is only between parts; a part's holes are not used (outer outline only); arcs are cut every 4°; a part with no closed outline is packed by its convex hull
