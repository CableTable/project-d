# Project D glossary

Project D's own words, based on the project docs and names in `web/`; `DECISIONS.md` wins over older descriptions, and planned features are marked below.

## App

**Add to layout**: Takes the prepared drawing into a layout as a part at its true size. Where: `web/app.js`.

**Apply**: Commits the current preview, which in Step and repeat turns the grid into separate placed parts. Where: `web/app.js`. Avoid: Save.

**Auto-nest**: Packs mixed parts by their actual outlines from the sheet's bottom-left, using allowed quarter turns and keeping gaps and grippers clear. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Auto-nest method and UI.

**Autosave**: Copies changed tabs into local recovery storage every two minutes without writing over drawing files. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Autosave and recovery. Avoid: Save.

**Background picture**: A dimmed, locked JPG, PNG or rendered PDF page beneath a drawing that can be scaled and measured but is left out of DXF Save and part PDF output. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Background, Part PDF and Autosave and recovery.

**Balloon**: A numbered inspection callout placed by hand on a drawing, PDF or picture, with its data saved in a separate balloon file. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Inspection balloons. Avoid: Dimension.

**Command alias**: A short word such as `tb` for Trace that is typed and accepted in the command bar. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Command bar and Shortcuts. Avoid: Single-key shortcut.

**Command bar**: The floating input near the cursor for commands, coordinates and clickable options, with full history available through F2. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Command bar. Avoid: Fixed bottom command line.

**Compare**: Overlays parts in different colours and can highlight outline differences beyond a chosen tolerance, worked out once when the Compare button is pressed (a move afterwards marks the result "out of date"). Where: `TODO.md`, item 41; `web/compare.js`.

**Contour**: Makes one or more new outlines inside or outside a closed outline at repeated distances, with sharp or rounded corners, on the Contour layer. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Contour tool and Contour colour and line style. Avoid: Trace.

**Cutter export (Export for cutter)**: Writes a separate DXF copy containing only LINE, ARC and LWPOLYLINE, flattening curves to a chosen tolerance and leaving out text, fills and other unsupported objects. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Cutter-safe DXF. Avoid: Normal Save.

**Dimension**: A saved measurement callout with lines, arrows and a value that does not count toward a part's size. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Kept dimensions and sizes and More dimension types. Avoid: Driving constraint.

**Dimension style**: A named preset for dimension appearance, including text height, arrow size, decimals and units. Where: `TODO.md`, item 27; `web/dims.js`.

**Drawing tab**: A tab for editing a drawing and preparing parts before putting them on a layout. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Layout page.

**Duplicate**: Makes copies of a placed part along a chosen direction, with distance measured edge to edge. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Duplicate and Duplicate both ways.

**Edge grip**: A handle at the middle of a selected rectangle's side. Dragging it moves that side along its normal, keeping a rectangle; Shift keeps proportions about the opposite side's midpoint. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Rectangle edge grips. Avoid: Gripper.

**Extra gap**: Adds room after one chosen row or column of the repeat grid and drops rows or columns that no longer fit. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Extra gap.

**Extract part**: Names the selected drawing geometry and saves it in the Parts library for reuse at the drawing's prepared scale. Where: `TODO.md`, items 5, 6 and 19; `web/app.js`.

**Fit**: Changes the view so the drawing or sheet fits in the window. Where: `TODO.md`, item 42. Avoid: Scaling the drawing; Fit to sheet.

**Fit to sheet**: Works out repeat rows and columns from the usable sheet area instead of using typed counts. Where: `TODO.md`, items 7 and 8; `web/layout.js`.

**Gap**: The edge-to-edge space between neighbouring parts in a repeat grid or nest. Where: `web/layout.js`, `DECISIONS.md`, Auto-nest UI. Avoid: Gripper.

**Grid**: The live rows and columns of copies generated by Step and repeat, which become separate placed parts when applied or edited. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Grid copies. Avoid: The drawing's snap grid.

**Gripper**: A reserved band in mm on any sheet edge that parts must keep clear of, with zero meaning none. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Gripper. Avoid: Grip; an extra margin added to a gripper.

**Grips**: Small handles on selected drawing geometry that move or stretch its points, centres or radius; none show when more than 200 shapes are selected (`MAX_SELECTED`), when their total handles exceed 5000 (`MAX_GRIPS`), or on the immediate Text to outlines result selection. Selecting those outlines again enables their grips within both limits. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Grips. Avoid: Grippers.

**Group**: A set of placed parts that selects, moves, turns and copies as one while keeping its members as parts. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Group and Ungroup. Avoid: DXF block.

**Guidelines**: Positioning guides dragged from rulers and used for snapping. Where: `TODO.md`, item 38; `web/guides.js`.

**Keep only this**: Deletes everything except the selected outline or geometry from the drawing. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Outline. Avoid: Extract part.

**Layout tab**: A separate tab holding a sheet, parts and their positions, grippers, gaps, marks and repeat settings, which reopen from its saved DXF. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Layout page; `TODO.md`, item 20. Avoid: AutoCAD paper-space layout.

**Padlock**: The W / H proportions control: locked scales both sides together; unlocked changes each side independently. It starts locked and applies to selection size or the whole drawing when nothing is selected. Where: Drawing size fields; `web/app.js`. Avoid: Layer Lock.

**Lock**: Prevents edits to a layer's objects on a drawing or movement of locked parts on a layout. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Objects tab; `TODO.md`, item 37. Avoid: Hide.

**Measure**: Picks points or geometry to report a distance, size or angle without adding a saved callout unless it is kept. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Measure and Dimensions; `web/drawing.js`. Avoid: Dimension.

**Micro piece**: An entity whose own path length is at most the Join gaps tolerance and whose end is within that tolerance of an entity longer than the tolerance. Join gaps removes these pieces before joining, reports the count and restores them with the joins in one Undo. A lone tiny entity stays. Where: `web/edit.js`; `DECISIONS.md`, Join gaps: micro pieces.

**Nest**: The packed arrangement of parts produced by Auto-nest, also the command name that opens its dialog. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Auto-nest method and UI.

**Nudge**: Moves selected placed parts with the arrow keys by a chosen mm step, or ten times that step with Shift. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Nudge.

**Objects tab**: The right-panel tree of layers and objects, or parts and their placements on a layout, used to find, select, hide and lock things. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Objects tab. Avoid: A separate drawing tab; Object Manager is the older feature name.

**Part**: A reusable piece of prepared drawing geometry at true size that can have many placements on a sheet. Where: `TODO.md`, items 4, 6 and 19; `web/layoutdoc.js`. Avoid: One placement of that part.

**Part | Layout views**: The former per-file switch between preparing a part and arranging it on a sheet, replaced by drawing tabs and separate layout tabs. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Part / Layout and Layout page. Avoid: Describing it as the current tab structure.

**Parts library**: The local collection of named parts with previews and sizes, stored inside the app for reuse and exported to DXF only when asked. Where: `TODO.md`, item 19; `web/partstore.js`. Avoid: A folder of source files; cloud storage.

**Placed part**: One positioned, rotated or mirrored copy of a part on a layout sheet. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Placed part: X, Y, W, H, Angle. Avoid: The library part itself.

**Preflight**: An optional drawing check for duplicates, open cut paths, tiny gaps, off-sheet parts, wide lines and live text that warns without blocking export. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Cleanup / Preflight placement and Preflight: warn, not block.

**Properties**: The panel for inspecting or changing selected objects' layer, colour, line type, outline width and supported shape values. Where: `TODO.md`, item 24; `DECISIONS.md`, Line width.

**Recovery**: Offers local copies of unsaved tabs after an interrupted run, with copies removed after a successful save or clean close. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Autosave and recovery and Recovery after a clean close. Avoid: A backup of original files.

**Remove duplicates**: Removes repeated or overlapping geometry on the same layer, plane and drawing space while keeping uncovered portions. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Duplicate / overlapping geometry. Avoid: Removing overlapping parts from a nest.

**Save**: Writes DXF while protecting the opened source path, so the first save of an opened file asks for an edited copy's name. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Save: the opened file. Avoid: Overwriting the imported original.

**Set length**: Makes a drawing or background true size by picking two points and entering their real distance in mm. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, 1:1 scale and Background.

**Shaping**: CorelDRAW-style boolean operations on the selected closed outlines (Weld = union, Intersect = the shared area, Trim = the last picked outline minus the others), giving closed polylines; outlines inside another count as its holes. Simplify keeps each outline's visible part after subtracting outlines in front. Front minus back / Back minus front keep the frontmost / backmost outline minus all others; later in the drawing means in front. Create boundary adds the union on the current layer and keeps the originals. Where: Modify menu, `WELD` / `SHAPETRIM` / `INTERSECT` / `SIMPLIFY` / `FRONTMINUS` / `BACKMINUS` / `BOUNDARY`; `web/shaping.js`. Avoid: Trim (the tool that cuts lines back to cutting edges).

**Sheet**: The physical output rectangle with a custom or preset width and height in mm, whose bottom-left is the layout origin. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Sheets and Open DXF. Avoid: Drawing extents; the whole canvas.

**Step and repeat**: Fills a layout with a regular grid of one part using rows, columns, rotation and gaps. Where: `TODO.md`, items 7 and 8; `web/layout.js`. Avoid: Auto-nest of mixed parts.

**Tiny gaps**: Nearly meeting outline ends found within a chosen tolerance and joined by moving the ends together. Where: `TODO.md`, item 46; `DECISIONS.md`, Preflight: warn, not block.

**Trace**: Converts a background bitmap's coloured regions into closed vector polylines at the picture's position and size, with Outline mode built and Centreline still planned. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Trace a bitmap. Avoid: DXF TRACE; Contour; OCR.

**Unit fix**: Multiplies or divides the whole drawing or background by 25.4 about 0,0 to correct an inch/mm size mistake without changing the unit header. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Unit fix. Avoid: Changing display units.

**Work area**: The background used to view and edit a drawing, independently set to Follow app, Light or Dark from the status bar or View menu. Its drawing colours and feedback adapt to the background; sheet paper and PDF / Print keep their appearance. Where: `web/workarea.js`, `DECISIONS.md`, Work area (TODO 96).

## Drawing / CAD

**Advance**: The font's distance to the next glyph, including spacing adjustments. TXO keeps this distance when skipping a missing glyph. Where: `web/textoutline.js`.

**Attachment point**: The top, middle or bottom and left, centre or right point of an MTEXT box placed at its insertion point. TXO keeps this anchor after wrapping. Where: `web/textoutline.js`.

**Composite glyph**: A font glyph made from other glyphs with their own offsets or transforms, such as Å or é. TXO expands its components into ordinary closed outlines. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Text to outlines: composite glyphs.

**Counter**: A hole inside a glyph, such as the centre of O or the ring above Å; retained as a separate closed outline with opposite winding. Where: `web/textoutline.js`.

**DXF text escape**: A sequence in TEXT such as %%d (°), %%p (±), %%c (⌀) or `\U+00E9` (é), decoded before rendering or TXO. Where: `web/drawing.js`, plainText.

**Glyph**: The shape and metrics a font supplies for a character or shaped character sequence. Where: `web/textoutline.js`.

**Missing glyph (.notdef)**: The font's index-zero replacement, often drawn as a box. TXO skips its path, keeps its advance and names the unsupported character and Unicode code point in the result. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Text to outlines: missing glyphs.

**Text to outlines (TXO)**: Replaces selected TEXT and MTEXT with closed polylines on their own layers and colours, keeping counters, placement and MTEXT wrapping, in one undo step. Names skipped glyphs and simplified formatting. Where: Modify menu; `web/textoutline.js`.

**Text run**: Consecutive MTEXT characters sharing height, width factor, oblique angle and bold face. Brace groups restore the previous formatting; TXO measures runs together when wrapping words. Where: `web/textoutline.js`.

**1:1**: A drawing or output size in which one mm of geometry represents one physical mm. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, 1:1 scale and Output. Avoid: Fit to window.

**3DFACE**: A DXF face whose edges can be shown by the 2D reader but which is left out of cutter export. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `web/dxf-cutter.js`.

**ACAD_TABLE**: A DXF table that the reader draws through its referenced block. Where: `web/drawing.js`.

**ARC**: A DXF circular arc with a centre, radius and start and end angles. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `web/dxf-write.js`.

**ATTDEF / ATTRIB**: DXF records for a block's attribute definition and an inserted attribute value, such as a title-block field. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `web/dxf-write.js`.

**Block**: A named set of DXF entities reused through INSERT references, including the blocks Project D uses for parts and its own dimensions. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Apply and More dimension types. Avoid: Layout group.

**Bounding box**: The smallest rectangle aligned with X and Y that contains an object's current shape or placement. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Placed part: X, Y, W, H, Angle; `web/geom.js`. Avoid: Actual part outline.

**Bulge**: The number stored at a polyline vertex that makes its next segment a circular arc instead of a straight line. Where: `web/drawing.js`.

**ByLayer**: Uses the containing layer's setting for an object's colour, line type or lineweight instead of an explicit object value. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Line width and Contour colour and line style; `web/drawing.js`.

**CIRCLE**: A DXF circle that cutter export represents as two arcs. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `DECISIONS.md`, Cutter-safe DXF.

**Cut layer**: Any layer whose name contains `cut`, ignoring case, used by Preflight to check path closure across connected ends. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Cut layer by name. Avoid: A special DXF layer flag.

**DIMENSION**: An imported DXF measurement entity drawn from its block, while Project D's own dimension callouts use INSERT blocks instead. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `DECISIONS.md`, More dimension types.

**DWG**: The planned AutoCAD drawing input format to be converted through LibreDWG and saved here as DXF. Where: `Project-D-scope.md`, section 4; `TODO.md`, Next sessions. Avoid: An already built reader.

**DXF**: The app's drawing and layout file format, currently written as R2000 ASCII with layout settings kept inside the file. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, File format and Save; `TODO.md`, item 20. Avoid: A private Project D file format.

**ELLIPSE**: A DXF ellipse or elliptical arc that cutter export approximates with a polyline. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `web/dxf-cutter.js`.

**Entity**: One top-level DXF object in the shown drawing space, with an INSERT counted once even if it draws many block members. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Kept dimensions and sizes and Layer and entity counts. Avoid: One rendered line segment.

**Explode**: Replaces selected polylines with separate LINEs / ARCs or selected INSERTs with their transformed members, one block level at a time. The pieces are selected; one Undo restores the originals. Where: Modify menu, `X` / `EXPLODE`; `web/explode.js`. Avoid: Ungroup (layout parts).

**Hairline**: The project's 0.05 mm outline-width preset and minimum physical stroke for PDF / Print. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Line width and Preflight Hairline. Avoid: Zero width; a fixed screen-pixel width.

**HATCH**: A DXF filled boundary using either solid colour or a line pattern, left out of cutter export. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `DECISIONS.md`, Cutter-safe DXF.

**IMAGE**: A DXF image reference that still counts as an entity even when the app draws nothing for it. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Layer and entity counts. Avoid: The app's imported background picture.

**INSERT**: A DXF placement of a named block with position, scale and rotation. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `web/dxf-write.js`. Avoid: A copy of every member as separate top-level entities.

**Layer**: A named collection of drawing entities with shared appearance and visibility settings, with drawing locks applied per layer. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Layers and Objects tab. Avoid: One physical film colour in every file.

**Line spacing factor**: Multiplies MTEXT's default baseline pitch of 5/3 of its text height. TXO retains the saved factor. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `web/textoutline.js`.

**LEADER**: A DXF arrowed annotation read from other drawings, while leaders made here use editable blocks on Dimensions. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Leaders. Avoid: An inspection balloon.

**LINE**: A DXF straight segment between two endpoints. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `web/dxf-write.js`.

**Line type**: The solid, dashed or dotted pattern of a stroke. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Contour colour and line style. Avoid: Outline width.

**Line width**: The physical outline thickness, edited in mm and saved through DXF lineweights or polyline widths. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Line width. Avoid: A line's length.

**LWPOLYLINE**: A lightweight DXF polyline holding connected straight or bulged arc segments, including rectangles drawn here. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Drawing tools: how they work; `web/drawing.js`.

**Model space / Paper space**: The DXF drawing and sheet spaces, with Project D showing model space unless only paper space is available. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Drawing render. Avoid: Project D's drawing-tab and layout-tab distinction.

**MTEXT**: A DXF multiline text entity with paragraphs, a wrapping box, attachment point and inline formatting. TXO turns its laid-out text runs into closed polylines in one undo step. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `web/dxf-write.js`, `web/textoutline.js`.

**MULTILEADER / MLEADER**: Imported DXF annotation names handled by the reader's multileader renderer. Where: `web/drawing.js`.

**No plot**: A layer flag preserved in DXF as group 290 set to zero so other CAD or cutter software knows not to output that layer. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Layer "No plot". Avoid: Hide.

**Offset**: Copies a supported line, arc, circle or polyline a chosen distance to a picked side. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Offset, Trim, Mirror. Avoid: Multi-step Contour.

**ORTHO**: Keeps point picks or supported moves horizontal or vertical like holding Shift. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Status bar and Move and copy by dragging.

**OTRACK**: Object-snap tracking that lines a pick up with points already acquired from drawing geometry. Where: `TODO.md`, item 21; `web/track.js`.

**Outline**: A connected chain of drawing edges selected together by clicking an edge, often used as a part's outer cut shape. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Outline. Avoid: Necessarily a single polyline.

**POINT**: A DXF point location that is left out of cutter export. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `web/dxf-cutter.js`.

**POLAR**: Angle tracking that offers dotted guides at set angle steps while picking points. Where: `TODO.md`, item 21; `web/track.js`.

**POLYLINE**: The older DXF polyline form with separate VERTEX records, distinct from LWPOLYLINE and sometimes carrying mesh data. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `web/dxf-write.js`.

**Snap**: Places a picked point on supported drawing features such as endpoints, midpoints, centres or the nearest point on a line. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Snap. Avoid: Grid fit.
**Nearest snap**: The lowest-priority snap: the closest point on a line, arc, circle, ellipse, polyline or spline under the pointer, drawn as an hourglass; ends, middles and centres win when they are in reach. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Nearest snap. Avoid: On-line snap.

**SOLID / TRACE**: DXF filled shapes handled together by the reader and left out of cutter export. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `web/dxf-cutter.js`. Avoid: Bitmap tracing for TRACE.

**SPLINE**: A DXF curve defined by control data and approximated by a polyline for cutter export. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `web/dxf-cutter.js`.

**TEXT**: A DXF single-line text entity, unlike MTEXT. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `web/dxf-write.js`.

**Tolerance**: The allowed distance used by a particular operation, such as joining tiny gaps, comparing outlines or flattening cutter curves. Where: `TODO.md`, items 41 and 46; `DECISIONS.md`, Cutter-safe DXF. Avoid: One global precision setting.

**VERTEX**: A child record storing a point and related data in an older POLYLINE. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `web/dxf-write.js`.

**VIEWPORT**: A DXF paper-space view record handled by the reader but outside the normal writer's supported set. Where: `web/drawing.js`, `web/dxf-write.js`. Avoid: A Project D layout tab.

**WIPEOUT**: A DXF masking boundary that covers geometry behind it in the renderer. Where: `web/drawing.js`. Avoid: Deleting the covered geometry.

**Wireframe**: An app-wide outline-only view with one-pixel coloured lines, no fills and no background pictures. Remembered for the app; saved drawings, PDF / Print and exports keep their normal appearance. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Wireframe; View menu and status bar.

## Screen printing

**All colours black**: Prints every artwork layer in black rather than its drawing colour for film output. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, PDF colour pages.

**Check print**: A scaled paper proof with a title block and a warning that it is not film. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, PDF page rules. Avoid: A 1:1 film master.

**Colour page**: One PDF page for each visible artwork layer when printing one page per colour, with Sheet and Marks excluded as artwork on layouts. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, PDF colour pages. Avoid: Automatic separation by every entity's colour.

**Crop marks**: Output marks showing where the sheet artwork should be trimmed. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Sheets and PDF. Avoid: Registration marks.

**Film**: The true-size artwork output used to prepare a screen, normally one film per print colour. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, Output and PDF colour pages. Avoid: A fitted check print.

**Job strip**: The job information strip added through the PDF dialog's Marks options. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, PDF export dialog.

**Mirror**: Flips the film page, including strip text, so it reads from the emulsion side, while tile labels remain unmirrored. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, PDF Mirror. Avoid: Mirror X or Y of one placed part.

**Negative**: Produces a black page with white artwork and marks. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, PDF colour pages.

**Registration marks**: Common marks repeated on the colour pages so the printed colours can be aligned. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, PDF colour pages and Marks default. Avoid: Crop marks.

**Tile**: One overlapping A4 or A3 page of a larger 1:1 output, used to assemble the full-size drawing. Where: `DECISIONS.md`, PDF page rules. Avoid: A repeat-grid copy.

## Workflow

**As-built board**: A local `mockups/R<n>_AB_*.html` board drawn from real screenshots after every UI build and kept as the record of the screen as shipped. Where: `CLAUDE.md`, How changes ship; `WORKFLOW.md`, As built. Avoid: A proposed mock-up.

**Brief**: The task file given to a worker with the branch, spec, allowed files, checks and expected reply. Where: `WORKFLOW.md`, Every brief says.

**Grill**: Rook's question rounds before medium or large work to settle choices and terms, recording terms here and decisions in DECISIONS.md. Where: `WORKFLOW.md`, Grill before building. Avoid: Code review.

**One PR per session**: All session changes use one branch and one pull request, with commits per change and a single merge after approval and green checks for the head commit. Where: `CLAUDE.md`, How changes ship.

**Bishop**: The family name for workers on Rakshith's second Claude account, launched through `claude2`. Where: `WORKFLOW.md`, Who does what. Avoid: Rook's main account. Old name: Pip (until 2026-10-21).

**Bishop daily cap**: The cumulative weekly allowance of 14% times the number of days since Monday's 15:30 IST reset, carrying unused allowance forward and requiring approval to exceed it. Where: `WORKFLOW.md`, Bishop's daily budget. Avoid: 14% of the five-hour window.

**Bishop-build**: The second-account builder for mock-ups, screenshots and overflow builds, with a fresh session for each build or fix round. Where: `CLAUDE.md`, House rules; `WORKFLOW.md`, Who does what.

**Bishop-look**: The Haiku worker that looks at screenshots and reports the visual findings in a few lines for Rook. Where: `CLAUDE.md`, House rules; `WORKFLOW.md`, Keeping Rook small.

**Bishop-review**: The read-only build review role, with separate fresh reviews for spec fidelity and code standards in their own throwaway worktrees. Where: `WORKFLOW.md`, Two reviews per build. Avoid: Knight review.

**Bishop-test**: The worker that downloads the released exe and drives it on the PC, including its native dialogs, using an isolated test profile. Where: `WORKFLOW.md`, PC test. Avoid: Browser-only checks of `web/`.

**R-boards**: The numbered local design rounds such as R11 or R14 used for proposed screens and their As-built records in the Project D Mockups canvas. Where: `WORKFLOW.md`, Mock-up and As built. Avoid: Files to commit.

**Retro**: A session-end review of commits, briefs and worker reports that suggests rule or process changes for Rakshith to choose before merge. Where: `WORKFLOW.md`, Retro at session end. Avoid: A code-fix round.

**Rook**: The main-account Opus lead who plans, writes briefs, chooses UI, reads short reports and handles pushes and merges. Where: `CLAUDE.md`, House rules; `WORKFLOW.md`, Who does what.

**Knight**: The Codex worker for feature and fix builds and web research, which tests and commits builds without pushing and has no review role. Where: `CLAUDE.md`, House rules; `WORKFLOW.md`, Who does what. Avoid: Bishop-build; reviewer. Old name: Rowan (until 2026-10-21).

**Pawn**: The Antigravity worker for quick web lookups in its own research folder. Where: `WORKFLOW.md`, Who does what. Avoid: Builder; reviewer. Old name: Scout (until 2026-10-21).

**Session**: A bounded round of Rook-led project work collected in one PR, while each worker build, fix or review uses its own fresh execution session. Where: `CLAUDE.md`, One PR per session; `WORKFLOW.md`, Session end. Avoid: One TODO item per PR.

**Usage gate**: The check of cached Rook, Bishop and Knight usage before launching a worker to choose available headroom, wait for reset or pause. Where: `WORKFLOW.md`, Usage gate; `DECISIONS.md`, Cached usage CLI. Avoid: A per-run money cap.
